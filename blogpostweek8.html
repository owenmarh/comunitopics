i'm going to use this post to talk about three thousand. watching it i am reminded of nathaniel dorsky's devotional cinema. this essay or short book or novella or whatever you want to call it moves toward describing a theory of cinema as what the author calls direct experience. at least that's how i remember it. turns out this essay came about when dorsky taught his first class. the structure of the piece mirrored that of his class, which he had named 'film in search of a language.'

i've now watched three thousand twice. the first time, i was distracted, my attention elsewhere. i saw the images but didn't hear the sounds - and so wasn't affected. the second time, i was fully immersed, lights off in the dark of my room with only screen glow in front of me. watching it this time gave me the feeling of watching someone else's memories, but not just the personal ones (the familial, the ancestral, carrying just as much weight). this time i watched the Inuktitut version, while the first time i watched the english dub. watching the piece in its language i felt i was watching something so sacred but somewhat lost.

at least based off what the term means to me, the elements of direct experience communicated through the film all emerged from the prevalence of diegetic sound. but diegetic as it pertains to this film is a funny thing because this film doesn't have one world to prescribe one language to; it has many worlds, all of which move on their own terms. but the rules that follow consistency throughout are those that emulate the sounds of experience, and in turn, the images feel like they come from the same world, both as the sound and ours. to clarify, catering to direct experience here does not mean conforming. instead, it is a means of letting go, following the same principles of living unavoidable when present. the most clear example of what i'm talking about is in the film, when it shifts to a 3-D model of an Inuk village, follows the principles of sound that would happen in real life if you were moving past and around a source of it.
